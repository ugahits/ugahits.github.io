About Ugahits

Promoting Western Uganda Music to the Next Level. Discover, download and connect with Ugandan music from anywhere in the world.

Welcome to Ugahits

Ugahits is an online music platform based in Western Uganda, created to promote, preserve and connect the world with the rich musical talent of Western Uganda.

Established in 2023 by popular Ugandan innovator Promota Enoch Ug, Ugahits was founded with a clear mission of taking Western Uganda music to the next level by giving talented artists a digital platform where their music can reach audiences across Uganda and around the globe.

Music lovers from anywhere in the world can use Ugahits to discover and download both new and old Ugandan music, while artists can create accounts, upload their music and share their work with a global audience.

Our Founder

Ugahits was established in 2023 by Promota Enoch Ug, a Ugandan innovator passionate about technology, creativity and the growth of Uganda's music industry.

Through Ugahits, his goal is to create greater digital opportunities for Western Ugandan artists while making their music easier to discover and access worldwide.

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote and preserve Western Ugandan music by providing artists with a reliable digital platform to showcase their talent and enabling music lovers worldwide to easily discover and access Ugandan music.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a leading digital home for Western Ugandan music, connecting talented artists with listeners around the world and helping Western Uganda's music industry grow, evolve and gain greater global recognition.

What We Do

At Ugahits, we bring artists and music lovers together through a simple and accessible online music platform.

🎵 Discover Music

Explore a growing collection of new and classic Ugandan music, with a special focus on music from Western Uganda.

🌍 Global Reach

We help Western Ugandan artists make their music available to listeners in Uganda and across the world.

🎤 Empower Artists

Artists can create accounts, upload their music and use Ugahits to showcase their talent to a wider audience.

🗂️ Preserve Music

By making new and old music available digitally, Ugahits contributes to preserving Western Uganda's musical heritage for future generations.

Our Commitment

We believe that Western Uganda has incredible musical talent, unique sounds and stories that deserve to be heard far beyond the region.

Ugahits is committed to building a platform where artists can be discovered, music can be shared and audiences can connect with the sounds and culture of Uganda.

Whether you are an artist looking to share your music or a music lover searching for your next favorite Ugandan song, Ugahits is here to connect you with the music.
