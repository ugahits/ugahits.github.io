About Ugahits

Discover the story behind Ugahits — a platform created to promote Ugandan music, empower artists and connect Ugandan sounds with listeners around the world.

Who We Are

Ugahits is an online music store based in Western Uganda that enables music lovers from across the globe to discover and download new and old Ugandan music.

Established in 2023 by Ugandan innovator Promota Enoch Ug, Ugahits was created with a mission of taking Western Uganda music to the next level.

We provide a platform where Ugandan singers, musicians and artists can create accounts, upload their music and share their creativity with audiences in Uganda and beyond.

From established musicians to upcoming talent, Ugahits aims to give Ugandan artists a digital home where their music can be discovered, appreciated and shared.

Our Mission & Vision

What drives Ugahits forward.

🎯 Our Mission

To promote Ugandan music, especially music from Western Uganda, by providing artists with a platform to showcase their work and helping music lovers easily discover and access Ugandan sounds.

🚀 Our Vision

To become a leading digital destination for Ugandan music, connecting talented artists from Uganda with listeners across Africa and the rest of the world.

Our Story

Ugahits was born from a passion for Ugandan music and a desire to give talented artists, particularly from Western Uganda, greater digital visibility.

In 2023, Ugandan innovator Promota Enoch Ug established Ugahits with the goal of creating an online platform where Ugandan music could reach audiences beyond local communities.

Today, the platform continues to focus on discovering new talent, preserving music and creating opportunities for Ugandan artists to share their work with the world.
