Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how Ugahits may collect, use, protect and manage your information.

Last Updated: October 1, 2026

Your Privacy Is Important

This Privacy Policy explains how Ugahits may collect, use, store and protect information when you visit our website, create an account, upload music, download content or use our services.

1. About This Privacy Policy

Ugahits is an online music platform based in Uganda that connects music lovers with Ugandan music and provides artists with opportunities to share their work online.

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through the Ugahits website, user accounts, artist services and other services operated by Ugahits.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use Ugahits, we may collect different categories of information.

Account Information

Such as your name, username, email address, telephone number and other information you provide when creating an account.

Artist Information

Artist name, biography, profile information, photographs, music files and other content submitted to your artist profile.

Usage Information

Information about how you interact with the website, such as pages visited, searches, downloads and general platform activity.

Technical Information

Information such as browser type, device information, IP address and technical logs may be collected for security and operation of the platform.

3. Information You Provide

You may voluntarily provide information when you:

  • Create a Ugahits account.
  • Create an artist profile.
  • Upload music or other content.
  • Contact the Ugahits team.
  • Submit feedback or enquiries.
  • Participate in promotions or platform activities.
  • Make a payment where paid services are available.

4. How We Use Information

Ugahits may use collected information for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Providing access to Ugahits services.
  • Publishing and managing artist profiles.
  • Processing music uploads.
  • Providing music search and discovery features.
  • Processing transactions where applicable.
  • Responding to customer support requests.
  • Improving the website and user experience.
  • Preventing fraud, abuse and unauthorized activity.
  • Maintaining platform security.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

5. Artist-Submitted Information

Artists who create profiles on Ugahits may choose to make certain information publicly available.

Public information may include an artist name, biography, profile photograph, location, music releases and other information intentionally published by the artist.

Artists should avoid publishing sensitive personal information that they do not want publicly available.

6. Music Uploads

Music and other files uploaded by artists may be processed and stored so that they can be made available through the Ugahits platform according to the artist's selected settings and applicable platform terms.

Artists are responsible for ensuring that their uploads do not contain personal information that they do not have permission to publish.

7. Cookies & Similar Technologies

Ugahits may use cookies and similar technologies to help operate the website and improve the user experience.

Cookies may be used for purposes such as remembering preferences, maintaining login sessions, understanding website usage and improving security.

Your browser may provide settings that allow you to manage or disable certain cookies. Disabling cookies may affect some website functionality.

8. Analytics

Ugahits may use analytics or similar technologies to understand how visitors use the platform.

Such information may help us understand traffic, identify technical problems and improve our services.

9. Payments

If Ugahits provides paid services, payments may be processed through third-party payment providers.

Payment providers may collect and process payment information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Ugahits should not be considered the storage location for sensitive payment-card information where such information is handled directly by an authorized payment provider.

10. Sharing of Information

Ugahits does not intend to sell personal information to third parties as a normal part of operating the platform.

Information may be shared where reasonably necessary with:

  • Service providers that help operate the platform.
  • Payment providers where transactions are processed.
  • Hosting and technical service providers.
  • Security and fraud-prevention providers.
  • Professional advisers where necessary.
  • Authorities where legally required.

11. Public Information

Information that you intentionally publish publicly on Ugahits, such as an artist profile, artist name, biography or uploaded music, may be visible to other users and visitors.

Do not publish private information on a public profile unless you are comfortable with it being publicly accessible.

12. Data Security

Ugahits will take reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or alteration.

However, no internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Data Retention

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, prevent abuse or comply with applicable legal requirements.

The period of retention may vary depending on the type and purpose of the information.

14. Third-Party Services

Ugahits may use third-party services for functions such as hosting, analytics, payments, communications, security or other technical services.

These providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

15. External Links

Ugahits may contain links to external websites, social media platforms or other services.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of websites that we do not operate. Users should review the privacy policies of external services before providing them with personal information.

16. Children's Privacy

Ugahits is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

Where applicable, users who are under the age required to independently consent to online services should use the platform with appropriate parental or guardian involvement.

17. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, which may include the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion where legally applicable.
  • Raise concerns about how your information is handled.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Requests may be subject to verification and any limitations or exceptions provided by applicable law.

18. Account Deletion

If you want to close your Ugahits account, you may contact us through the available support channels.

Some information may need to be retained where required for legal, security, accounting or other legitimate purposes.

19. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Ugahits may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the platform, technology, services or legal requirements change.

Changes will be published on this page and the "Last Updated" date will be revised where appropriate.

20. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is intended to operate in accordance with applicable laws and regulations governing privacy and data protection in Uganda, together with any other mandatory requirements applicable to the relevant user or service.

21. Contact Ugahits

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal information, please contact us.

Email: info@ugahits.com

Location: Western Uganda

You can also use our Contact Us page to send an enquiry.

Your Privacy Matters

We want Ugahits to be a platform where artists and music lovers can enjoy Ugandan music while understanding how their information is handled.
